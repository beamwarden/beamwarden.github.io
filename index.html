> Beamwarden is the authoritative control plane for the Beamrider fleet. It manages identity, telemetry, deployments, SSH trust, and configuration for all Beamriders.

> Beamwarden provides a unified, deterministic, and secure management layer for all Beamrider devices. It centralizes trust, orchestrates deployments, ingests telemetry, and maintains the canonical identity of every node.
