Ride the Beam

Any sensor. Any data. One platform.

> If your team collects data from hardware in the field, Beamwarden is the control plane that manages it. A Beamrider node hosts your sensor stack — whatever it measures — and streams authenticated, structured telemetry to Beamwarden. Fleet identity, liveness, deployments, and configuration are managed centrally. Beamforge delivers live rolling statistics and historical analytics without additional infrastructure.

Radiation
CPM, CPS, dose rate from Geiger tubes or scintillators.
Environmental
Temperature, humidity, pressure, air quality, methane, VOCs, light, sound.
Vibration / IMU
3-axis accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer — structural or motion sensing.
Position / GNSS
GPS coordinates, altitude, velocity — mobile and geofenced deployments.
Atmospheric
Wind, precipitation, barometric trend, UV index.
Custom
Any numeric payload. Beamwarden stores and surfaces it — no schema required in advance.

Beamrider-0003 — Live Node — Multi-Sensor Stack
