Use Case

Multi-Sensor Environmental Monitoring

> A facility team needs continuous environmental telemetry across a building — temperature and humidity for compliance, vibration for early equipment fault detection, ambient light for occupancy, and sound levels for safety thresholds. They need one system that ingests all of it, maintains device identity, and surfaces live and historical analytics without requiring a data engineering team to stand it up.

> A single Beamrider node hosts the full sensor stack. It authenticates to Beamwarden with a hardware-bound bearer token, streams multi-channel payloads over HTTPS, and reports liveness continuously. Beamforge surfaces live rolling statistics and historical trend analysis — no additional infrastructure, no custom integrations.

Beamrider-0003 — Live Node — Operational

Temperature / Humidity
HS3003 via I²C. Calibrated readings for environmental compliance and HVAC correlation.
Accelerometer
3-axis vibration and tilt. Early indicator of equipment stress, motor imbalance, or structural events.
Ambient Light
Lux readings for occupancy inference, daylight harvesting, and anomaly detection in sealed environments.
Sound Level
Continuous dB monitoring for safety threshold alerting and equipment noise signatures.
