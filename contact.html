BEAMWARDEN

Ride the Beam

> Beamwarden is the authoritative control plane for the Beamrider fleet, offered as a fully‑managed PaaS or as a foundation for bespoke development and monitoring solutions. It provides a unified, deterministic, and secure management layer for our customers’ distributed sensing fleets, handling identity, telemetry, deployments, SSH trust, and configuration across every node.

> Beamriders are fully customizable remote‑sensing platforms—modular edge devices built to host whatever sensors, radios, or local logic a mission demands. Instead of acting as generic “hands and eyes,” each Beamrider becomes a purpose‑built field instrument, gathering high‑fidelity environmental data and enforcing Beamwarden’s identity, trust, and deployment model at the edge.

> Beamforge is the analytical engine of the Beamwarden ecosystem, transforming raw telemetry into the operational intelligence that powers our customers’ fleets. It refines the environmental and situational data collected by Beamriders into trends, anomalies, and actionable insight, giving organizations a clear, real‑time understanding of the world their distributed sensors are observing.
